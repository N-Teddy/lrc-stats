# LRC Stats V4: Strategic Intelligence & Asset Governance

## Phase 1: Predictive Analytics & AI Integration
- [ ] **AI Operational Forecaster**: Predicting future attendance based on historical cycles and event types.
- [ ] **Anomaly Detection Layer**: Automatically flagging unusual drop-offs in specific segments before they become "Drop Risks".
- [ ] **Natural Language Query**: "Ask" the dashboard for stats (e.g., "Show me the top 10 most active members this month").

## Phase 2: Advanced Asset & Logistic Oversight
- [ ] **Equipment Tracking**: Dedicated module to track mission-critical items (radios, vests, kits) assigned to personnel.
- [ ] **Inventory Lifecycle**: Status tracking for consumables (Medical kits, supplies) linked to operations.
- [ ] **QR Asset Sync**: Generate QR codes for physical assets for instant mobile identification.

## Phase 3: High-Fidelity Communication & Engagement
- [ ] **Unified Announcement Hub**: Send operational alerts or mission updates directly from the dashboard.
- [ ] **Engagement Gamification**: Visual "Achievements" or "Ranks" for personnel based on participation milestones.
- [ ] **Member Portal Lite**: A read-only web view for members to check their own stats and upcoming schedule.

## Phase 4: Enterprise Security & Mission Integrity
- [ ] **Encrypted Data Vault**: Hardware-accelerated encryption for the local tactical database.
- [ ] **Audit Trail 2.0**: Immutable log of all administrative changes (Who edited whom and when).
- [ ] **Multi-Endpoint Sync**: Redundant syncing to multiple cloud providers simultaneously (S3 + Dropbox + Google Drive).
