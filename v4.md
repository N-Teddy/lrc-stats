# LRC Stats V4: Community Connection & Group Harmony

## Phase 1: Caring Insights & Trends
- [ ] **Participation Trends**: Identifying shifts in involvement to help identify who might need a friendly check-in.
- [ ] **Gathering Analysis**: Understanding which types of activities (Meetings, Community Events, Socials) resonate most with the group.
- [ ] **Simple Info Assistant**: A quick way to ask for summaries, like "Who has joined us for most gatherings this month?"

## Phase 2: Community Resource & Venue Management
- [ ] **Loaned Resources**: A simple way to track shared items (books, materials, equipment) borrowed by members.
- [ ] **Venue Directory**: Managing the different locations where we gather, including maps or special instructions.
- [ ] **Quick Check-in**: Use QR codes for faster attendance marking at larger communal gatherings.

## Phase 3: Growing Together & Communication
- [ ] **Communal Notice Board**: A central place within the app for shared announcements and updates for the group.
- [ ] **Milestone Highlights**: Automated reminders for member anniversaries, birthdays, or special participation milestones.
- [ ] **Personal Involvement View**: A simple area for members to see their own history and upcoming group activities.

## Phase 4: Privacy & Group History Protection
- [ ] **Safe Data Vault**: Enhanced protection for our shared community records on the local computer.
- [ ] **Record History**: Keeping a simple log of updates to ensure our group's history stays accurate and clear.
- [ ] **Reliable Multi-Sync**: Ensuring our collective history is never lost by syncing to multiple safe backup locations.
